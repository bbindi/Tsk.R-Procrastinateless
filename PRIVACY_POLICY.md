# Privacy Policy for Tsk.R: Procrastinateless

**Effective Date:** July 31, 2026  
**Developer:** Brixavier (`com.brixavier.tskr`)  

### 1. Overview
Tsk.R: Procrastinateless ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the App. Tsk.R is designed as an offline-first application, meaning your personal task data remains local to your device.

### 2. Information Collection and Storage
* **Local Task Data:** All task titles, due dates, notes, and checklist items created within Tsk.R are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local SQLite/Room database. We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal task data on external cloud servers.
* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

### 3. Permissions Used
To provide core functionality, Tsk.R requests the following Android system permissions:
* **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Required to trigger time-sensitive task reminders and Mission Mode notification alerts precisely when due.
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Required to display upcoming task warnings and countdown alerts on Android 13+.
* **Vibration & Audio:** Required to trigger haptic feedback and alarm audio during scheduled reminder events.

### 4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
* **No Third-Party SDKs:** Tsk.R does not incorporate third-party analytics, tracking framework SDKs, or advertising networks. 
* **Offline-First Operation:** The App does not transmit data over the network during normal operation.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
Tsk.R does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general productivity tool intended for users aged 13 and older.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
