## 1. Introduction

Tsk.R: Procrastinateless ("Tsk.R" or "the App") is a productivity application designed to help users manage tasks, reminders, plans, and personal notes.

Tsk.R follows an **offline-first approach**. Your personal productivity data is stored locally on your device rather than on a cloud server operated by the developer.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how third-party services may process information, and what choices are available to you.

## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### Local Task and Productivity Data

The App stores the following information in its private application storage:

- Task titles, descriptions, and due dates.
- Personal notes and plan steps.
- Checklist items and related task information.
- App preferences and settings.
- Other information you enter to manage your productivity.

This information is stored locally using Android's Room database, backed by SQLite, and other private application storage where appropriate.

**The developer does not operate a cloud service that receives or stores your productivity content.** Your task titles, notes, plans, and checklist contents are not intentionally transmitted to the developer or the advertising SDK.

### Personal Accounts

Tsk.R does not require account registration or sign-in to use its core productivity features.

The developer does not operate an account system or collect account-registration information through the App.

## 3. Android Permissions

Tsk.R uses Android permissions and system capabilities to support its functionality.

- **Internet and network access:** Supports Google AdMob advertisements and privacy-consent functionality.
- **Alarm scheduling:** Schedules task reminders and time-sensitive alerts.
- **Notifications:** Displays reminders and related alerts, subject to Android permissions and settings.
- **Vibration and audio:** Provides alert feedback when applicable.
- **Screen and alarm-related capabilities:** Supports relevant Mission Mode and alarm behavior where permitted by Android.

The availability and behavior of some features depend on your Android version, system settings, and the permissions you grant.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

### Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Tsk.R uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Although your productivity information is stored locally, the advertising SDK may automatically collect and share certain information with Google and relevant advertising partners. Depending on the SDK's operation and configuration, this information may include:

- **Approximate location:** Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location.
- **Device or advertising identifiers:** Such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, where applicable.
- **App interactions:** Information about app launches, taps, ad views, and other interactions.
- **Diagnostics:** Technical and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related purposes associated with the advertising service.

**Your task titles, personal notes, plans, and checklist contents are not intentionally sent to Google Mobile Ads by Tsk.R.**

For further information, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

### Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Tsk.R uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage advertising privacy consent where required.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, the SDK may present a consent message or privacy choices. The consent process may involve communication with Google to retrieve or record relevant consent information.

When available, you can access **Settings → Privacy Choices** within Tsk.R.

Your available choices depend on applicable consent requirements and the configuration of the advertising service. Changing your privacy choices may affect how advertisements are served, but may not disable every form of data processing necessary to operate the service.

### Google Play Services: In-App Updates and Reviews

Tsk.R uses Google Play services to check for app updates and offer users the opportunity to submit a review through Google Play.

These features communicate with Google Play as needed to provide their respective services. Tsk.R does not intentionally send your task titles, notes, plans, or checklist contents through these features.

Information you submit directly to Google Play, such as a store review, is handled under Google's applicable policies.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Tsk.R and its integrated services is used for the following purposes:

- **Productivity functionality:** To create, store, organize, and display your tasks, reminders, plans, notes, and checklists locally.
- **Reminder delivery:** To schedule and display reminders and related alerts.
- **Advertising:** To request and display advertisements.
- **Consent management:** To present and apply relevant advertising privacy choices.
- **Analytics and diagnostics:** The advertising SDK may process technical information and app interactions for its documented purposes.
- **Security and fraud prevention:** The advertising service may use relevant information to detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.
- **App updates and reviews:** To check for updates and offer access to Google Play's review functionality.

The developer does not operate a backend service for collecting or analyzing your personal task content.

## 6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell your task titles, personal notes, plans, or checklist contents to third parties.

However, information collected by integrated third-party services may be transmitted to or shared with Google and relevant service providers or advertising partners as necessary for their services.

In particular, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the information described in Section 4.

These third parties process information under their own applicable privacy policies, terms, and data-handling practices. The developer does not control every aspect of their independent processing.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

### Productivity Data

Your productivity information remains in the App's private storage until you delete it or remove the relevant application data.

You can delete information using the App's available features. You can also clear the App's storage through Android settings or uninstall Tsk.R.

**Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it can permanently remove locally stored tasks, notes, plans, preferences, and other app data.**

If you want to preserve information, make any necessary arrangements before clearing the storage or uninstalling the App.

Because the developer does not operate a cloud database for your productivity content, the developer generally does not possess a separate server-side copy of that content to retrieve or delete.

### Third-Party Data

Information collected by Google Mobile Ads or other third-party services is subject to their respective retention, deletion, and privacy policies.

Deleting local App data does not necessarily delete information that a third party has already collected.

For further information, visit [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 8. Data Security

Tsk.R stores productivity information in private application storage provided by Android. This helps restrict direct access to the App's local data by other ordinary applications.

Communications performed by Google's advertising services are subject to the security measures used by those services. Google documents that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your device protected and use Android's available security features.
